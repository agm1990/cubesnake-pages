Privacy Policy

Cube Snake 2048 · Effective date: October 17, 2026

Cube Snake 2048 ("the Game", "we") is a free, offline single-player game supported by advertising. This policy explains what information is processed when you play and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information ourselves. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no forms. We do not run our own servers or analytics.
  • Game progress (scores, coins, gems, unlocked skins, settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. Deleting the Game deletes this data.
  • The AI opponents in the arena are computer-controlled; there is no online play or chat.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Game shows ads through Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:
  • Device identifiers, including the Advertising Identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking;
  • Advertising data (ads shown, ad interactions) and product interaction data;
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • Crash, performance and other diagnostic data.
Google uses this information as described in its policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. App Tracking Transparency

On first launch, iOS asks whether the Game may track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the IDFA is not shared and you will see non-personalized ads. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

4. Your choices and US state privacy rights

  • You can limit ad personalization through the iOS tracking setting above, and reset your Advertising Identifier in iOS settings.
  • Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws (such as the CCPA/CPRA) may have the right to know, delete, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising. Because we do not hold any personal information ourselves, you can exercise these rights by declining tracking, through Google's controls at adssettings.google.com, or by contacting us below.
  • We do not sell personal information for money.

5. Children

The Game is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads in the Game are limited to content suitable for general audiences. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data security and retention

Game progress stays on your device until you delete the Game. Information processed by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows the latest version.
