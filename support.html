How to play

Drag anywhere to steer. Eat cubes smaller than your head — the numbers add up. Bigger heads win head-on collisions; bite an opponent's tail to take that segment. Fly lifts your whole snake out of reach for 3 seconds, Boost speeds you up, and Catch pulls the nearest flying snake back to the ground.

FAQ

Is it online? No. All opponents are AI; the game works offline (ads need a connection).

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

An ad didn't give me a reward. Rewards are granted only after the ad finishes. If an ad fails to load, try again with a stable connection.
